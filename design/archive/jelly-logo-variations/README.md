# Jelly: standalone jellyfish logo variations

The jellyfish-shaped j is retired. The wordmark is regular lowercase “jelly”, with an ordinary dotted j. The icon is a separate, optional symbol inspired by the jellyfish emoji 🪼.

Six raster logo explorations, generated individually using built-in ImageGen:
- [01 / Emoji soft](01-emoji-soft.png) — Stay closest to the 🪼 jellyfish emoji. A naturally domed pale-cyan blue translucent bell, a gently scalloped rim with a subtle lilac underside, four long delicate sinuous tendrils. Front-facing with slight organic asymmetry. Soft restrained emoji-style volumetric shading, luminous material but absolutely NO outer glow. NO face or eyes. Airy, graceful and light, beautiful silhouette.
- [02 / Blue stamp](02-blue-stamp.png) — A highly simplified flat ONE COLOR electric blue #1672F8 jellyfish emblem. Broad smooth half-dome bell, gently scalloped lower edge, THREE short thick undulating tentacles separated with generous white gaps. A confident compact silhouette, no face or eyes, no internal details, no gradient, no shading. Bold enough for a favicon. The tentacles flow downward and do NOT hook into a letter. Delight through soft geometry.
- [03 / Lilac bell](03-lilac-bell.png) — A minimal dimensional emoji-inspired jellyfish with a pearlescent pale periwinkle bell and richer blue-violet inner rim. Five soft ribbon tentacles of staggered lengths; restrained purple-to-blue shading on the creature only. Dome wider than tall, softly arched top, elegant centered front view. NO face or eyes. Sophisticated iOS emoji-like polish, a small sculptural symbol rather than a character toy.
- [04 / Flowing line](04-flowing-line.png) — An elegant crisp blue outline jellyfish symbol, round-ended consistent medium-weight strokes. One simple domed bell contour with a scalloped lower edge, THREE sinuous tentacle strokes drifting diagonally to the right as if in a gentle current. Very few lines and plenty of white space. Flat vector-like appearance, no shading, no gradient, no face or eyes. A calm fluid emblem, strong enough stroke thickness at small size. Not a letter or monogram.
- [05 / Friendly bell](05-friendly-bell.png) — A softly dimensional sky-blue jellyfish character distilled into a logo. Compact rounded bell, THREE short rounded wavy tentacles, two TINY navy dot eyes with one microscopic highlight each, no mouth, no cheeks, no accessories. Delicate cyan shading like the 🪼 emoji, not the full plush avatar previously explored. Simple and compact, with a gentle personality. The eyes are understated, most of the character comes from the rounded silhouette.
- [06 / Floating ribbon](06-floating-ribbon.png) — An original two-tone flat blue jellyfish mark inspired by the 🪼 emoji drifting in water. Tilt the entire creature about 12 degrees clockwise. A solid cobalt smooth dome with a pale cyan crescent at its bottom rim, FOUR elegantly curved short ribbon tentacles fanning softly to the left, two cobalt and two sky blue. No eyes, no face, no glow or gradients. Strong clean flat silhouette. Playful movement without extra motion lines.
