# Standalone Jelly logo explorations

Built-in ImageGen. The wordmark uses regular lowercase lettering with an ordinary j; the jellyfish is a separate symbol inspired by 🪼.

## 01 / Emoji soft

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE refined standalone jellyfish logo concept for the friendly AI chat app Jelly. Inspiration is the jellyfish emoji 🪼: domed translucent bell and dangling wavy tentacles. This is a jellyfish symbol, NEVER a letter j, NEVER a monogram. Do not construct any letters from tentacles. Do not copy the previous jellyfish-j wordmark.
A square 1024x1024 identity card on completely OPAQUE PURE WHITE background. Top 70%: one large standalone jellyfish mark, centered, with generous clear space and complete tentacles, roughly 430px tall. Lower 25%: the SAME mark repeated at small app-header scale to the left of the ordinary word "jelly". The word uses a conventional bold rounded sans-serif such as SF Pro Rounded, all lowercase, standard unmodified j with a normal ROUND DOT and normal hook, normal e l l y, navy #14243A. Icon and text are clearly separate with generous gap; wordmark exactly jelly, no extra letters. Bottom discreet small gray concept label as specified. Small icon must remain recognisable at 24-32px. No extra drawings, no hardware, no gradients on background, no glow, no black backdrop, no watermark, no slogans. Studio-level craft and optical balance.
VARIATION: Stay closest to the 🪼 jellyfish emoji. A naturally domed pale-cyan blue translucent bell, a gently scalloped rim with a subtle lilac underside, four long delicate sinuous tendrils. Front-facing with slight organic asymmetry. Soft restrained emoji-style volumetric shading, luminous material but absolutely NO outer glow. NO face or eyes. Airy, graceful and light, beautiful silhouette.
Bottom label exact text: "01 / Emoji soft".
