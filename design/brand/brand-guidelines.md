# Brand guidelines

Use the approved Friendly bell next to a normal lowercase wordmark. Primary lockup on white/icy blue, inverse on navy. Keep at least one quarter of the visible bell width as clear space. Minimum visible mark height 24 px; prefer the simple silhouette for small reproduction. At product header size, the mark sits in a 42 × 44 px box with 32 px Nunito 900 lettering.

Never stretch the mark, replace the j, add a mouth, change the number of tentacles, or animate the logo continuously. Keep decorative sea details outside reading and input areas. Avatars may sit in circular pastel fields; do not use colour or species as the sole status indicator.

Brand blue `#1672F8`, cyan `#83D7F7`, navy `#14243A`. The accessible light UI action colour is `#0964DA`; dark UI uses `#86BDFF` with dark ink. Native system sans for product text; Nunito only for brand lettering. Code and filesystem paths use system monospace.

Voice: warm, brief, useful. State what happened and what can happen next. “Your draft is safe.” “Choose a folder on the Jelly server.” “Existing agents keep their working directories.” Avoid nautical names for ordinary controls, errors, or security actions.
