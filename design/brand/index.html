Leave room to float.

Clear space: at least ¼ of the visible bell width on every side. Keep the standalone mark at least 24 px high; use the simplified mark below 32 px.

Use the regular Nunito 900 wordmark. Never replace the j with a creature.

Friendly, never busy.

White space first. Icy blue surfaces, readable navy type, one strong blue action. Sea details belong in identities, empty states, and quiet edges.

Use the dark lockup only on a dark surface. Never stretch, add a mouth, or put the mark behind message text.
