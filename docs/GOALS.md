# Goals

Jelly goals are persistent objectives with an actual Pi continuation loop, not a todo list. Open the **+** menu at the bottom-left of the chat composer and choose **Goal**. The same menu offers **Add File** for uploads. **Goals** also remains available from the target icon beside the agent's options, including newly created agents. Save an objective, then choose **Resume goal** to authorize autonomous execution. Saving does not call a model. Browser disconnection does not stop execution; Jelly must remain running. There is no token-budget setting.

## Behavior

- One current goal per Jelly agent, independent of cwd and Pi session identity. States: `paused`, `active`, `blocked`, `completed`; clearing retains a `cleared` archival record but removes the current goal.
- Edit only while stopped with no admitted goal run. Editing preserves goal identity and progress and leaves it stopped. An open editor pins its create/edit operation, goal identity and base revision. Live refreshes or conflicts never silently rebase a draft; cancel and reopen explicitly. Completion evidence likewise pins its original goal and revision; clear the evidence to start a new draft. Completed goals are immutable except Clear. Clear before creating a replacement; wait for old goal work to finish first.
- Resume requires an idle, unarchived agent with no pending human messages or admitted goal work. It uses normal Jelly admission and current settings/context. There is **no hidden wait queue or retry timer**. Busy conflicts are visible; unavailable credentials persist a blocked reason. Resolve the issue and explicitly resume.
- Every normal action-capable Pi request gets one current goal system envelope through `context_with_system`, including ordinary tool follow-ups, human steering, native idle child wakes and requests after compaction. It supplies the full active objective/progress or generic stopped instructions. Neither the internal compaction cap/count nor the former token budget is included. Pi's leading prompt and unrelated tool declarations remain intact.
- At `agent_before_settle`, the extension composes preceding extension drafts and requests exactly one further turn only when the bound execution is active and successful, with no queued messages/other continuation and no active or unknown owned child work. Native child completion/heartbeat notifications remain the sole child wake source. Lost completion reconciliation retains a terminal job until its notice is consumed; its in-flight parent response stays tracked but does not itself block the next goal continuation. Other live/unknown children and unconsumed notices still block continuation. The goal never relaunches children automatically.
- `jelly_goal` is registered only in the goal's parent session. `get` inspects state; `progress`, `block`, and `complete` require a nonempty summary (completion should include requirement-by-requirement evidence). The tool cannot create, resume or edit objectives. Its model payload is an explicit objective/progress/state projection, never the internal Goal record or operator-only stop reason. Ordinary child missions remain package-owned recovery records, not mirrors of Jelly goals.
- Completion requires an explicit tool or operator action. A finished run or final answer is **not** completion. Model completion is an attestation, **not an independent verification**. Genuine blockers may be reported immediately; no prompt-level three-turn audit is imposed.
- Goals can continue across many compactions. A fixed internal safety policy stops after **100 successful parent-session compactions per explicit admitted resume**. There is no 32-continuation breaker. Normal Jelly run limits are **10,000 model turns and 24 hours**, including non-goal runs; goals do not evade them by silently starting another run.
- Errors and unexplained settlement block the goal. Human queue/steering input fences execution authority and pauses active goals. Resume explicitly afterward. Schedules share normal run admission and cannot overlap or displace active goal work.

### Pause, Stop and the compaction boundary

Pause, Complete and Clear fence future **goal-generated** continuations. They do not roll back files, retract already-admitted context/requests, abort tools or kill children. The panel separately displays **work still running**, with **Stop agent**. A boundary continuation committed before Pause is admitted work. Native queued notifications and tool follow-ups may still finish; there is no competing goal wake queue.

**Stop** fences goal authority before aborting the normal parent/child lifecycle. A later callback cannot reactivate it. Objective edits and resume must wait for admitted work to finish. Fresh Session preserves the goal without resuming it. Archive is allowed only after work stops and cannot silently reactivate a goal on restore.

The **automatic compaction safety stop is stronger than Pause**: let the hundredth compaction finish successfully—do not interrupt or cancel its summarization. Its completed summary/checkpoint, ledger entry and paused reason commit together, then the parent and owned-child lifecycle are stopped before the next normal model request. No further normal goal provider request or continuation is allowed, including queued/native wakes. Already-completed effects cannot be rolled back. This is never success; the operator sees the reason and must explicitly Resume after cleanup. Only a newly admitted explicit resume resets the compaction count. Editing, compaction, failed admission, receipt replay and restart do not reset it.

Only successful parent compactions from the bound goal/run/epoch count; manual, threshold and overflow compactions share this boundary. Failed/cancelled attempts, delegated-child and nested provider work do not count. A successful split-turn compaction counts once even if its summarizer makes multiple calls. Successful entry identities deduplicate callbacks; stale epochs cannot alter a replacement goal or newly resumed execution. Token telemetry remains in normal Pi transcripts, but there is no goal token accounting or token-based stop policy.

### Restart safety and migration

Graceful shutdown pauses active goals before cancellation. On restart, previously running Jelly runs become interrupted and active goals become paused with an uncertainty warning. Even activation committed just before a crash requires explicit resume. **No accepted tool/request is automatically replayed.** Review external effects and retained child records before resuming. Already-stopped and completed goals remain stopped, and a compaction-limit reason remains visible.

Schema v19 migrates v18 current records without losing goal identity, objective, progress or history. Former active, exhausted and blocked records become paused for inspection, never auto-started. Old run snapshots, usage ledgers and mutation receipts remain historical evidence, not authority. Receipts still reject changed-payload reuse and prevent any old Resume from launching work again. An exact legacy request receipt may be replayed read-only; new create/edit requests reject obsolete budget fields and all counter/limit overrides.

## Implementation and API

`src/server/goals.ts` owns state transitions. SQLite schema v19 adds `goal_compactions` to the existing `agent_goals`, `goal_runs` and `goal_mutations` records. `(runId, entryId)` deduplicates successful compactions, with goal identity and epoch checked against admission. Checkpoint, count, paused state and event commit in the same SQLite transaction before cancellation or event publication. Persistence failure rolls back and stops the execution rather than allowing another request. Restart never replays compactions or requests.

`src/server/goal-extension.ts` is an inline Pi extension passed through `DefaultResourceLoader.extensionFactories`, **not installed in shared settings**, so detached children do not inherit a Jelly goal owner. No package-owned mission files or installed modules are changed. Initial/resumed execution uses the normal Jelly admission transaction before provider/tool work. Identity + epoch fence mutations and continuation; progress updates do not change the epoch. Operator changes invalidate it. Durable unique `(goalId, epoch)` admission and normal run uniqueness prevent duplicate execution.

Pi 0.87.1's supported `session_compact` hook runs after the successful entry is appended. Its event can incorrectly identify an older entry when summary strings repeat, so Jelly resolves the new active-branch compaction relative to the preparation snapshot. Replayed success callbacks without a new preparation are ignored. The hook commits before calling supported non-awaiting `ctx.abort()`; the host starts `Harness.stop()` without awaiting idle inside the hook and joins cleanup during finalization. Pi can still call a provider with an already-aborted signal after between-turn compaction, and resets its abort flag after pre-prompt compaction. A composed public `Agent.prepareRequest` guard therefore checks latched run-local goal-safety and normal run-limit flags before and after the previous request hook, reasserting non-awaited cancellation and throwing a generic stopped error before provider invocation. Reassertion prevents Pi from starting recovery compaction after resetting its abort flag. The normal turn/deadline fence applies to goal and non-goal runs, including expiration during pre-prompt compaction; ordinary Pause does not set these flags.

Goal events publish after commit and use existing authenticated resumable SSE/snapshot refresh. Full objectives are not copied into every-agent sidebar snapshots. The panel serializes/coalesces reads across SSE invalidations, mutation/conflict responses and Stop; reads spanning a mutation are discarded and a fresh post-settlement read resolves ordering. Delayed mutation bodies never overwrite a newer view. Failed requests retain pinned drafts and retry IDs.

Pi's standalone compaction calls bypass normal context hooks. `session_before_compact` adds a bounded objective/progress snapshot to each nonempty history/split-turn span, preserving Pi's summarizer, preceding extension data and cut points. This is summarization context, **not authorization**. Pausing during summarization may make it historical; the next normal request rereads authority. Request-only hygiene removes Jelly-owned obsolete continuation/budget messages and goal sections, replaces legacy full goal tool records/descriptions with the current safe projection, and excludes generated admission prompts matched to durable goal-run provenance. Projected compaction/branch summaries and the independent `preparation.previousSummary` input also redact recognizable generated legacy budget spans without dropping adjacent evidence or changing durable summary originals. Raw history and human messages remain evidence; human text is not stripped merely for mentioning budgets or goals. Hygiene also applies to subsequent ordinary/Fresh Session runs so restored generated instructions cannot authorize old policy.

Compatibility boundary: the envelope guarantee uses Pi's normal structured-prompt path. In installed 0.87.1, a forced entire system prompt (`before_agent_start.systemPrompt` / `forceSystemPrompt`) is projected **after** context hooks and discards sections. A preceding forcing extension blocks goal continuation with a visible reason without rewriting its prompt or aborting admitted work. A later forcing handler or arbitrary trusted context rewrite cannot be made safe by this adapter. No private host patch or provider wrapper is installed.

All routes share Jelly's control-cookie, CSRF and same-origin enforcement:

| Method | Route | Body / result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GET | `/api/agents/:agentId/goal` | `{goal, runningRunId}` |
| POST | `…/goal/create` | `{requestId, objective}`; creates paused |
| POST | `…/goal/edit` | `{requestId, goalId, expectedRevision, objective}` |
| POST | `…/goal/pause`, `…/goal/resume`, `…/goal/clear` | `{requestId, goalId, expectedRevision}` |
| POST | `…/goal/complete` | same plus nonempty `summary` |

Mutation responses are the current `{goal, runningRunId}` view. Stable request IDs deduplicate identical mutations; changed payload reuse and stale revisions return 409. Replaying a successful Resume never launches again. Wrong membership returns 404. Unknown/server-owned fields are rejected for new mutations. Objectives/summaries are bounded to 16,000 characters, request IDs to 100. CRUD needs no credentials. The existing 64 KiB request limit applies. There is no new planner, scheduler, execution-history API, user-configurable limit or independent verifier.

## Research and reuse decision

Research was supplied from pinned upstream source inspection, not inferred from plugin names or an old local Codex checkout.

- **Codex** reference: [`openai/codex@0ddee3e1d898f7823df97e9aaf08b420be51c804`](https://github.com/openai/codex/tree/0ddee3e1d898f7823df97e9aaf08b420be51c804), a researched main revision, **not a released-version compatibility claim**. Key sources: [`spec.rs`](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/0ddee3e1d898f7823df97e9aaf08b420be51c804/codex-rs/ext/goal/src/spec.rs), [`continuation.md`](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/0ddee3e1d898f7823df97e9aaf08b420be51c804/codex-rs/ext/goal/templates/goals/continuation.md), [`budget_limit.md`](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/0ddee3e1d898f7823df97e9aaf08b420be51c804/codex-rs/ext/goal/templates/goals/budget_limit.md), [`accounting.rs`](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/0ddee3e1d898f7823df97e9aaf08b420be51c804/codex-rs/ext/goal/src/accounting.rs), [`runtime.rs`](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/0ddee3e1d898f7823df97e9aaf08b420be51c804/codex-rs/ext/goal/src/runtime.rs). Jelly adopts one persistent objective, explicit status, automatic idle continuation and audited completion guidance. It does **not** claim Codex parity for child accounting, automatic restart, blocker audits or provider usage-limit classification.
- **Installed pi-subagents 0.71.0** ([mission docs at its revision](https://github.com/nicobailon/pi-subagents/blob/4af5e85a427b9f87334585ae8d0eb365d4dd2a1e/docs/missions.md)): inspected installed `docs/missions.md`, `src/missions/goal-driver.js`, `src/extension/control-notices.js`. Goal notices use `triggerTurn: details.source === "async"`; goal source is `"goal"`, so native mission goals **do not trigger another turn**. Ownership is Pi-session/project scoped; budget sums linked children, not parent work. No public host mission-management API solves Jelly's per-agent lifecycle. Keep the existing mission/run ledger for delegated-work recovery unchanged, rather than using it as an incompatible goal runtime.
- Existing dedicated plugins were researched: [`pi-goal@0.1.7`](https://github.com/Michaelliv/pi-goal/tree/3f100be5434454d3388755a119d01caca9127c16) has a real follow-up loop but cache-inclusive accounting, weaker cancellation and old namespace peers. [`@astrosheep/pi-goal-next@0.2.6`](https://github.com/astrosheep-zero/pi-goal-next/tree/f82e160832af2eddaa1b8178dd7f15e4f398dac6) has useful journaling/fencing ideas; it documents Pi ≥1.1.0 and reads `agent_settled.aborted`, absent from Jelly's 0.87.1 host. That is an abort-semantics mismatch, not necessarily a load-time failure.
- **[`@fyeeme/pi-goal@1.0.6`](https://registry.npmjs.org/@fyeeme/pi-goal/-/pi-goal-1.0.6.tgz)** is the strongest reuse candidate: it uses the same actionable `agent_before_settle` boundary and the same `input + cacheWrite + output` arithmetic. Its declared Pi ≥0.99.0 peer alone does not establish incompatibility. Its session-branch persistence, broader assistant/tool/compaction accounting and fallback follow-up ownership do not replace Jelly's per-agent durable admission, web idempotency, explicit restart authorization and native child-wake owner. Its evaluator completes when unavailable, and its subprocess resolver can select Jelly's server entrypoint rather than Pi. Those ownership and evaluator risks—not version numbers alone—justify retaining Jelly's small inline adapter. Focused request-context hygiene and boundary draft composition are adopted without importing another scheduler/evaluator.
- `pi-goal-x@0.34.1` adds a larger workspace multi-goal pool, scheduler and audit owner, also reading newer `agent_settled.aborted`; its input/output formula omits separately normalized cache writes. **No new dependency or host upgrade**, independent verifier or stagnation detector. No upstream prompt/source code is copied wholesale.
- Installed Pi **0.87.1** documentation and examples: complete `docs/sdk.md`, `docs/extensions.md`, `docs/compaction.md`, `docs/session-format.md`, `docs/message-types.md`, `docs/sessions.md`; SDK examples `01-minimal.ts` through `13-session-runtime.ts`; linked extension examples `hello.ts`, `todo.ts`, `dynamic-tools.ts`, `truncated-tool.ts`, `custom-compaction.ts`. Exact boundary contracts checked in `dist/core/extensions/types.d.ts` and `dist/core/agent-session.js`. The supported actionable hook is `agent_before_settle`, not notification-only `agent_settled` or premature `agent_end`. Normalized usage verified in `@earendil-works/pi-ai/dist/api/openai-responses-shared.js` and `anthropic-messages.js`.

## Validation and remaining limits

`bun run test:goals` uses deterministic local providers with installed Pi, not real user goals or paid calls. Regressions cover 100 consecutive successful compactions with identical summaries; the exact 99/100 boundary; no post-limit provider invocation/continuation or queued/native wake; explicit resume reset; failed/cancelled attempts; dedupe, stale epochs, transactional rollback, reopen and v18 migration; no generated numerical policy in model/tool/summarizer payloads; more than 32 continuations; objective-only UI/API; and SIGKILL recovery without replay. They retain child-reconciliation, every-request authority, forced-prompt compatibility, preceding extension drafts, split-turn compaction, operator races, client draft pinning and async read-ordering tests. Server regressions cover 10,000-turn/24-hour bounds.

Trusted extensions/tools retain broad permissions. Prompt rules and model completion are not independent enforcement. Native child cancellation cannot roll back external effects, and unconfirmed cleanup still requires operator inspection. Existing ambient goal plugins could compete; do not install another driver for the same work. There is no total cost/spend cap, automatic restart/wake, rollback or independent verifier. Real-provider and visual browser smoke checks remain deployment-specific; no live Jelly restart or hosting change is part of this implementation.
